## The run

You leave an apartment stairwell at nightfall and cross one transit-side district to the river
crossing before it is sealed. Route 4 is blocked; the pharmacy and the parking structure both lead
around the wreck to the underpass and the crossing plaza. The affected are residents altered by the
event: slow to jogging, hostile at close range, and only dangerous when they reach you.

- **Ammunition is scarce.** Six rounds loaded, none spare at the start. Every shot is heard.
- **Sprinting is loud.** Walk near the affected; sprint to break contact.
- **Health does not regenerate.** Field dressings heal a little; a dressing plus antiseptic makes a full first-aid kit (Items → Combine).
- **Saving.** Crossing a checkpoint autosaves. The radio in the pharmacy back room saves to any slot.
- **Documents.** Read notices and notes where you find them; read ones are kept under Items.

## Offline and installation

The game installs as a web app (Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone; Install app on Android and
desktop Chrome/Edge) and plays offline after the first load. Append `?fresh=1` to the address to clear
every cache and reload.

## QA overlay

F9 or a three-finger tap toggles the debug overlay (frame time, resolution, input source, service
worker state, scene, navigation mode). F10 or the overlay button draws grounding rays and the navmesh.
